Privacy Policy for Raw Trusted

Effective: April 13, 2026

Raw Trusted does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

All card photos you open in the app are processed on your own device. Photos are never uploaded to a server. The app has no login, no user accounts in this version, no analytics, and no tracking.

Grade estimates and prices shown in the app are unofficial predictions provided for information only. Raw Trusted is not affiliated with PSA, Beckett, or any grading company, and an estimate is not a guarantee of the grade a grading company will assign.
